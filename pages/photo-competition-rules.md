## Photo Competition rules:

1.⁠ ⁠Each Participant may only submit 3 photos per week

2.⁠ ⁠A participant who won the prior week’s photo competition may only submit 1 photo the next week.

3.⁠ ⁠Participants many only submit photos taken in the week for which they are submitting said photo (Your accountability is before God on that one ;) )

4.⁠ ⁠You may crop photos but may not tamper with any other settings (e.g. sharpness, contrast, vignette, brightness, saturation, warmth, or use any AI to adjust the photo) (again your accountability is before God ;) )

5.⁠ ⁠Submissions are due by Thursday at Maghrib, and the winner will be announced the next day (or over the weekend if its too hard to decide)

6.⁠ ⁠Submissions must be made by messaging me (Zakariya, see the SJC isoc chat for my number) with accompanying text elucidating the location at which they were shot (e.g. “the backs, St John’s” or “the river Cam, by the University Centre”)

7.⁠ ⁠The winner of the weekly photo competition shall receive a sweet in their pigeon hole as a reward, and get their photo on the instagram (ok that last part is not up to me to decide)

8.⁠ ⁠The winner may also remain anonymous if they wish (if they express this when submitting their entry)

9.⁠ ⁠If there are any people in focus as the subject of your submission you must first have their permission before submitting the picture in reference.


## Criteria for winning photos, biases and disclaimers:

As someone who has written many a treatise on beauty, I think I’m a relatively qualified judge, but I do have some biases. For impartiality’s sake I shall not partake in the competition. A winning submission is above all else purposeful. Having a beautiful subject like an exotic animal or beautiful building, doesn’t mean you will win. Credit is given for skill, an eye for hidden beauty, the ability to capture meaning in a shot, and aesthetics. 
In terms of biases I will admit that I have an extremely strong, inextinguishable attraction towards gothic architecture, but merely featuring a beautiful building doesn’t make a good photo, and I shall try to mitigate this bias.

I also possess quite strong iconoclastic tendencies, (for obvious religious reasons). That doesn’t mean that you can’t submit a photo in which people are the subject, but I generally perceive nature to be far more beautiful than people.

The most beautiful photo is by no means the most eye-catching (as elucidated here [On Beauty](https://notzakariya.github.io/2026/07/16/On-Beauty/)  ), and so ideally the most beautiful photo wins each week ;)
